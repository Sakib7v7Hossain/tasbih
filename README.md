# Tasbih

A simple, private dhikr counter and daily dua tracker for Android. It works fully offline, has no ads and no account, and collects no data.

**Version:** 1.0.0 · **Requires:** Android 7.0 (API 24) or newer · **Languages:** English, বাংলা, Türkçe

---

## Download

**[⬇ Download the latest APK](https://github.com/Sakib7v7Hossain/tasbih/releases/latest)**

On the page that opens, scroll to **Assets** and tap `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`. It is the 64-bit build for almost every phone made in the last several years. If the app does not install, your phone is very old: open an Issue and I will share a "universal" APK.

### How to install

1. Download `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk` from the Releases page to your phone.
2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to **install unknown apps**.
3. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, choose **Install anyway**. The app is not on the Play Store yet, so Google has not seen it before.
4. Open **Tasbih**.

To update later, download the newer APK and install it over the old one. Your duas and counts are kept.

## What it does

- **Tasbih counter:** a large, easy tap area with 33 beads, a golden burst when a round finishes, and tap sounds (soft, wood or off). You can also count with the volume buttons: up counts, down undoes.
- **Your own duas:** add any dua or dhikr with its own target count.
- **Today's duas:** pick which duas you do each day, tick them off, and see a short "MashaAllah" message when you finish all of them.
- **Progress:** today's count, a 7-day view, your day streak, rounds and your all-time total.
- **Salah times and alarms:** set your own prayer times and an alarm for each prayer, with the next prayer shown on top.
- **Home screen widget:** see today's count without opening the app.
- **Keep screen on** while counting, vibration feedback, and light or dark mode.

## Privacy

- Everything is stored **only on your phone**.
- The app has **no internet permission**, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
- No ads, no tracking, no sign-in.
- Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

## Permissions, and why

| Permission | Used for |
|---|---|
| Vibration | Tap feedback while counting |
| Notifications, exact alarms | Salah alarms at the times you set |
| Full-screen alert, wake lock | Showing the alarm when the screen is off |
| Foreground service (media) | Playing the alarm sound |
| Start after reboot | Bringing your alarms back after the phone restarts |
| Ignore battery optimisation (optional) | Stops the system from delaying alarms |

On some phones (Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Samsung), you may also need to allow **Autostart** and set battery use to **Unrestricted** for Tasbih, or alarms may be late.

## Help

- **"App not installed":** an older copy signed with a different key may be on the phone. Uninstall it, then install again.
- **Alarm did not ring:** open Settings, turn on notifications and exact alarms for Tasbih, then check the battery note above.
- **Salah times:** you set them yourself on the Salah tab (tap a prayer to change its time), so they match your local mosque.
